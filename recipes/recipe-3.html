Lasagna

Ingredients:

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions:

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  2. In a large pan, cook the ground beef until browned.
  3. Add the marinara sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. In a bowl, mix together the ricotta and egg.
  5. Assemble the lasagna by layering the noodles, meat sauce, and cheese mixture.
  6. Bake in a preheated oven at 375°F for 30 minutes.
