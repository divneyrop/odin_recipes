Spaghetti Carbonara

Ingredients:

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 100g pancetta
  • 2 large eggs
  • 50g grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to package instructions.
  2. In a pan, cook the pancetta until crispy.
  3. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs and Parmesan cheese.
  4. Drain the spaghetti and return it to the pot. Add the pancetta and egg mixture, stirring quickly to create a creamy sauce.
  5. Season with salt and pepper, and serve immediately.
